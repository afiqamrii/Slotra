# Pro roles and audit

Every active member retains baseline `organization:view` so the venue workspace can render. It grants no settings mutation, financial report, or cross-branch access; all other custom permissions are explicit allowlist entries.

The five predefined roles remain OWNER, ADMIN, MANAGER, STAFF, and VIEWER. Pro may add a named custom role with explicit permissions grouped in the UI by Bookings, Customers, Payments, Resources, Pricing, Reports, Members & Staff, Settings, Integrations, and Billing. A custom role's allow-list replaces its base role's grants; an inactive or unavailable custom role fails closed. OWNER cannot be reassigned through custom-role or branch-assignment actions. Only an owner/admin with `member:update` and all-branch scope may create or assign roles. The actor may grant only permissions they themselves hold, and cannot change their own role or branch scope. The role assignment service checks tenant membership and the role's tenant inside a transaction.

`organization_members.branch_scope` is `ALL` or `SELECTED`. Selected assignments are in `member_branches` with composite tenant foreign keys. For selected members, a sensitive service must pass an authorized branch ID to `authorizedMembership`; an older organization-wide service fails closed. Team-access changes, booking operations, branch changes, pricing changes, manual payments/refunds, API keys, webhooks, domains, branding, and corporate actions record safe Pro audit entries where wired. Audit records contain summary and small structured before/after details, not secrets or full webhook/payment payloads. The viewer is tenant-scoped, filterable, and limited to 50 rows per page.

This audit stream is not yet comprehensive for every historical or Business-domain mutation (for example every package and promotion edit or invitation lifecycle). It starts when Pro audit logging is enabled; it does not reconstruct past events. Additional coverage, retention policy, and immutable external archival need a separate hardening pass.
