# Pro corporate accounts

An organization can create an active corporate account with company/billing contact, optional terms, monthly credit limit in minor currency units, and an explicit overage flag. Staff approve existing organization customers as corporate users; an email address alone does not authorize corporate spending. Corporate booking remains a normal customer booking and uses the existing availability, pricing, status, and conflict engine. The staff-assisted form is the current entry point. Public self-service employee identity verification is not implemented.

`createCorporateBooking` locks the tenant-owned account row before creating the booking and charge in one outer transaction. It validates the approved customer and checks total applied charges for the account's month before commit. Two RM80 bookings cannot spend a RM100 limit, even on different resources. The period starts on the first day of the month in the organization's IANA timezone. Cancellation marks the charge `REVERSED` in the booking transaction and releases allowance; it does not issue a refund. A failed credit check rolls back both charge and booking. The booking's payment balance remains outstanding until actual payment is recorded—corporate credit is a billing obligation, not collected cash.

Corporate reports show charged booking value and count, not invoices, recognized revenue, or profit. No automatic invoicing, payment collection, tax calculation, ERP integration, employee SSO, or public employee checkout exists yet. A production two-connection PostgreSQL race test remains required in addition to isolated PGlite coverage.
