# Starter operational plan

Starter is **RM79/month** and positioned as **“Run your bookings.”** It includes 200 first-confirmed bookings per temporary UTC calendar month, one active branch, up to ten non-disabled courts/spaces, one owner, and one non-owner staff seat. Guest public booking, staff and walk-in booking, calendar, customer records, basic pricing/hours, blocks, manual/pay-at-venue payment tracking, basic dashboard/reports, and CSV are in scope.

Professional is **RM129/month**, positioned as **“Understand your business.”** It inherits Starter and includes 1,000 first-confirmed bookings/month, one branch, 20 non-disabled courts/spaces, one owner, three non-owner staff seats, online payment/deposit/refund entitlement, Professional analytics, filtered reports/CSV, and scheduled-report configuration. Business is **RM179/month** with 3,000 bookings; Pro is **RM279/month** with 10,000. Higher tiers inherit Professional reporting entitlements, but Business/Pro domain features are not implemented. A nonproduction **one-time ToyyibPay sandbox test** can switch a Starter venue to Professional after verified test payment; this is not a real subscription, renewal, or live purchase.

Starter booking usage is one immutable record at first CONFIRMED status, even if later cancelled. A temporary 10% grace (configurable 0–20%) permits new bookings past 200 through 220, without marketing that overage as included. At the hard stop the owner sees a plan-action message and public customers see a respectful unavailable venue. New resources and invitations are blocked when their caps are reached; existing data is retained.

Booking email uses a transactionally inserted notification record and post-commit delivery. A missing customer email produces `SKIPPED`. Without a configured sender, development/test produces `DEV_PREVIEW` and production records `FAILED`; neither is claimed as delivered. `RESEND_API_KEY` and a verified `EMAIL_FROM` are required for real booking email. Provider failure cannot roll back a successful booking. A scheduled retry worker and production sender/domain verification remain deployment prerequisites. Production account verification, password-reset email, and staff invitation delivery still use the older development-only path and are **not production-ready**.

The dashboard and reports use booked-value and net-paid snapshot fields; they do not implement an accounting ledger or cash-flow statement. The basic activity chart and customer list are operational views, not Professional analytics. No live online gateway or subscription charge is offered on Starter.

Professional's online capability requires a connected **venue-owned production merchant provider**; no such live provider is implemented. ToyyibPay sandbox and TestProvider move no real money. Scheduled-report email likewise requires Resend configuration plus a protected scheduled runner; without them the UI does not claim delivery. See [REPORTING.md](REPORTING.md) for the exact metrics and current limitations.

The sidebar beneath the Slotra logo shows the current plan; the mobile navigation panel shows the same badge. Settings → Plans offers an owner-only **Test upgrade · RM129 sandbox** action when the shared ToyyibPay sandbox is enabled. The bill uses the sandbox category for testing only. A successful browser redirect never activates the plan by itself: the server verifies bill code, amount, external attempt reference and provider transaction before changing `organizations.plan_code`. The plan assignment then persists for testing; there is no monthly renewal, expiry, proration, downgrade, cancellation, invoice, or real-money subscription. Business/Pro remain preview-only.
